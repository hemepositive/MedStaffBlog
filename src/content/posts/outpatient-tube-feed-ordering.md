<div class="key-message">

When home tube feeding is anticipated, place an inpatient Registered Dietitian (RD) consult early so recommendations are available before discharge.

</div>

## The 4-step workflow

1. **Identify & document** — Provider identifies the need for home tube feeding and documents the clinical reason.
2. **Consult RD** — RD assesses nutrition needs and documents the recommended formula/regimen, hydration, and monitoring.
3. **Complete & sign** — Provider reviews the RD recommendation, completes the appropriate Outpatient Tube Feeds order, and signs it.
4. **Coordinate** — Case Management/Social Work routes the signed order and coordinates vendor, payer, equipment, and discharge logistics.

## If the patient chooses ContinuumRx

When a patient chooses ContinuumRx for enteral therapy, the Detailed Written Order (DWO) is completed and signed as follows:

1. **Intake** — Nicole Simmons (ContinuumRx) emails the HH Clinical Nutrition team at <grp_dietitian_clinical_nutrition.main.wc@hhsys.org>, copying ContinuumRx Intake and Brian Buel.
2. **RD completes the DWO** — The HH Dietitian completes the Enteral Detailed Written Orders (DWO) form, places it on the chart, and replies to the email that it is done.
3. **Social Work is alerted** — ContinuumRx messages the Social Worker in CarePort that the Dietitian has completed the DWO.
4. **Send for review** — The Social Worker sends the DWO to ContinuumRx via CarePort.
5. **ContinuumRx checks accuracy** — ContinuumRx reviews the DWO and messages in CarePort when it is correct.
6. **Provider signs** — Once ContinuumRx approves it, the Social Worker asks the physician/provider to sign the DWO.
7. **Return signed DWO** — The Social Worker sends the signed DWO to ContinuumRx via CarePort.

<div class="takeaway">

**RD recommends. Provider orders and signs. Case Management/Social Work coordinates.**

</div>
