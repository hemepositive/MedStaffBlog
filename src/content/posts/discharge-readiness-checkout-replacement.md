<div class="key-message">

The obsolete Discharge Readiness/Checkout component is causing a serious allergy clinical decision support (mCDS) error. It is being replaced on **Wednesday 10/28**. This will affect most providers.

</div>

## What is happening

During discharge medication reconciliation, if you prescribe a medication the patient has a documented allergy to, the mCDS screen appears **blank (white)** when you sign. You cannot complete or finalize the prescription.

This can delay medication availability and discharge, and it may affect timely patient care. The cause is the continued use of the obsolete Discharge Readiness/Checkout component, which is producing backend errors.

## What is changing

Because of the safety impact, IT is replacing the component on an accelerated timeline. The Discharge Readiness/Checkout component is deprecated and is being replaced by separate links marked with a vertical red asterisk within the respective components.

![The Discharge Readiness component, with its Medication Reconciliation, Patient Education, Follow-Up and Discharge Documentation items, replaced by separate red-asterisk links for Problem List, Patient Education, Follow Up, Order Profile and Home Medications](/images/discharge-readiness-checkout-replacement/discharge-readiness-replaced-by-red-asterisk-links.png)

## Who is affected

This change will affect most providers, including:

- All surgeons
- Women's health, including OB-GYN
- Gastroenterology
- Cardiothoracic surgery
- Pain management
- Pediatrics

## What to do

Starting **Wednesday 10/28**, use the red-asterisk links instead of the Discharge Readiness/Checkout component when discharging patients.

<div class="takeaway">

If you see a blank white allergy screen when signing a discharge prescription, you are hitting this bug. Contact the IT help desk at your hospital so the patient's prescription can be completed.

</div>

If you have any problems, please reach out to the IT help desk at your hospital.
