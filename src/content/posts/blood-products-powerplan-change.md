## What is changing

On **November 2, 2026**, blood products will be ordered from the new **HOSP Blood Products PowerPlan**. The old PowerPlan will be discontinued on that date, so use the new one for all blood product orders going forward.

The new PowerPlan is organized into subphases. You select the subphase that matches what the patient needs, instead of working from one long list of orders.

## How to order

1. **Check the blood bank status.** In the Related Results to the left of the PowerPlan, double-click **Type and Crossmatch** (the T&S/T&C result) to open the report. It shows the patient's current blood bank status and which products are currently available for the patient.
2. **Use the Blood Bank Status table** at the top of the PowerPlan to decide what to order (see below).
3. **Select a subphase** by checking the box next to it.

![New HOSP Blood Products PowerPlan with the Blood Bank Status table, the subphase list, and the Type and Crossmatch result opened by double-clicking](/images/blood-products-powerplan/new-powerplan-subphase-ordering.png)

## What to order, by blood bank status

| Blood bank status | What to order |
|---|---|
| No labs collected / ordered, needs blood **now** | Type & Cross + Transfuse |
| No labs collected / ordered, might need blood **eventually** | Type & Screen |
| Current Type & Screen (T&S), needs blood **now** | Add Additional Units of Blood + Transfuse |
| Current Type & Cross (T&C), needs blood **now** and no previous units given | Transfuse only |
| Current Type & Cross (T&C), all previous units given / more units needed | Add Additional Units of Blood + Transfuse |
| Outdated T&S or T&C, needs blood **now** | Type & Cross + Transfuse |

## The three subphases

- **HOSP Blood Products Make Available** — The patient may need blood or products eventually, but does not need transfusion now.
- **HOSP Blood Products Transfuse Now** — The patient needs a blood product transfused now. Determine the current blood bank status before placing the appropriate lab or order(s).
- **HOSP Additional Units for Blood** — Additional blood needs to be prepared using the existing blood bank sample/crossmatch. Transfusion may occur now or may wait for further labs or clinical assessment.
