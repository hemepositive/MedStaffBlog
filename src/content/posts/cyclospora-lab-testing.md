The HH Lab has updated lab testing for Cyclospora. A **Cyclospora and Isospora stain** test is now orderable on stool specimens and will be performed in house Monday–Friday, day shift.

<div class="key-message">

**The standard Ova and Parasites (O&P) test does not detect Cyclospora.** You must search "Cyclospora" to find and order the new test, as shown below.

</div>

## Accepted samples

- Fresh stool in a sterile collection container (1–5 g), refrigerated
- The O&P collection kit (pink and grey top vials), at room temperature

## Ordering

Search "Cyclospora" when placing the order — do not rely on the O&P test to detect it.

![Order search screen showing "Cyclospora and Isospora" among the results when searching "cyclos"](/images/cyclospora-lab-testing/cyclospora-order-search.png)

<div class="takeaway">

To test for Cyclospora, search "Cyclospora" and order the new **Cyclospora and Isospora** stain on a stool specimen. The routine O&P test will not detect it.

</div>
