The Alerts/Rules Review & Optimization Workgroup (ARROW) has heard your feedback and is pleased to announce that we are removing two of the most voluminous pop-ups for most Cerner positions.

## What's changing

### Latex Allergy pop-up alert

The Latex Allergy pop-up alert is transitioning to a passive SmartZone alert for most users. The pop-up alert will continue for non-physician staff in peri-op areas, cath lab, and pharmacy.

### Renal Function pop-up alert (radiology orders)

The Renal Function pop-up alert when ordering radiology studies is being discontinued. It hasn't proven as valuable as we anticipated when we went live with 1Chart/Cerner.

<div class="takeaway">

If you have any questions or concerns, ARROW would be happy to walk through the reasoning behind these changes. We're monitoring alert volumes and will continue improving user experience and patient safety through high-quality alerts.
