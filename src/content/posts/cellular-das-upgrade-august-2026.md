The 1st floor of the hospital and the 1st floor of Blackwell Medical Tower should be good now — all repairs and upgrades to those areas are complete. This includes:

- Cellular service on the 1st floor of Blackwell Medical Tower

- The Hospitalist work areas in the old Medical Records space

If you experience any issues in these areas, please let us know.

## In progress: floors 2 and above

The other areas of the hospital are still in progress. Infrastructure repair to the DAS has been completed up through the 6th floor, but we are waiting on new amplifiers and updated firmware for the equipment to complete the work for floors 2 and above.
