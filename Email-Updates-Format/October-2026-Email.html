Discharge Readiness is replaced on Wednesday, October 28
An error in the obsolete component is blanking the allergy alert screen when you sign discharge prescriptions.
  • The problem: if you prescribe a medication the patient has a documented allergy to during discharge medication reconciliation, the mCDS screen appears blank (white) and you cannot finalize the prescription.
  • The change: Discharge Readiness/Checkout is replaced by separate links marked with a red asterisk (*): Problem List, Order Profile, Home Medications, Patient Education and Follow Up.
  • Who is affected: most providers, including all surgeons, OB-GYN, GI, cardiothoracic surgery, pain management and pediatrics.

New HOSP Blood Products PowerPlan starts Monday, November 2
The old PowerPlan will be discontinued. Choose a subphase based on blood bank status and whether blood is needed now.
  • Check the status first: double-click Type and Crossmatch in Related Results to see the patient’s current blood bank status and available products.
  • Three subphases: Make Available (may need blood eventually), Transfuse Now, and Additional Units for Blood (uses the existing crossmatch).
  • Use the Blood Bank Status table at the top of the PowerPlan to see what to order, then check the box next to the matching subphase.

How to complete post-acute care orders at discharge
A screenshot walkthrough of the Discharge page for patients going to assisted living, a skilled nursing facility, home health or home with DME.
  • Place HOSP Discharge Orders, then select the sub-order-set that matches the destination: Assisted Living, Home Health, Skilled Nursing (SNF) or DME Orders.
  • Finish every red-asterisk section until each shows a green check mark.
  • For discharge to any facility, sign both the discharge summary and the discharge med list.
