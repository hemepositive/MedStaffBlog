**ARROW update — two high-volume pop-up alerts scaled back**
The Alerts/Rules Review & Optimization Workgroup (ARROW) is removing two of the most voluminous pop-ups for most Cerner positions.

* The Latex Allergy pop-up is transitioning to a passive SmartZone alert for most users (it continues for non-physician staff in peri-op areas, cath lab, and pharmacy)
* The Renal Function pop-up on radiology orders is being discontinued — it hasn't proven as valuable as anticipated

Details: [https://medicalstaffnews.com/arrow-alert-optimization-sept2026/](https://medicalstaffnews.com/arrow-alert-optimization-sept2026/)

**Wound photography — always include a size reference**
A quick best-practice reminder for anyone documenting wounds with photos.

* A photo without a size reference has no scale — camera distance and angle change apparent size, so the same wound can look larger or smaller from shot to shot
* A sterile gauze square or paper measuring guide in the frame lets anyone reviewing the chart judge true size and track change over time
* Photography supports your written measurements — it doesn't replace charting length, width, depth, and tissue characteristics

**Bladder training / catheter clamping — skip it before Foley removal**
An evidence brief on a practice that's still common but not supported by the data.

* Clamping short-term indwelling catheters (≤7 days) before removal offers no benefit and is associated with higher UTI risk and longer time to first void
* Remove the catheter promptly once it's no longer needed, using passive balloon deflation
* Follow with a voiding trial — expect a spontaneous void within ~6 hours and check post-void residual by bladder ultrasound

Details: [https://medicalstaffnews.com/bladder-training-catheter-clamping/](https://medicalstaffnews.com/bladder-training-catheter-clamping/)

**Outpatient tube feed ordering — provider quick guide**
A 4-step workflow and role breakdown for ordering home enteral nutrition.

* Place the inpatient RD consult early, as soon as home tube feeding looks likely, so recommendations are ready before discharge
* RD recommends the formula/regimen; the provider reviews, completes, and signs the order; Case Management/Social Work coordinates vendor, payer, and equipment
* Before discharge, confirm the RD consult is done, documentation supports the need, and the signed order is with Case Management/Social Work

Details: [https://medicalstaffnews.com/outpatient-tube-feed-ordering/](https://medicalstaffnews.com/outpatient-tube-feed-ordering/)
